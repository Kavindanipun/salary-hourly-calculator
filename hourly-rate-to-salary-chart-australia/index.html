How to convert hourly rate to salary

The basic formula is simple:

Annual salary = Hourly rate × Hours per week × Weeks per year

Using the 38-hour weekly reference and 52 weeks in a year, the calculation becomes:

Annual salary = Hourly rate × 1,976

Example: $30 per hour × 38 hours × 52 weeks = $59,280 gross per year.

$45 an hour is how much a year in Australia?

Using 38 hours per week and 52 weeks per year, $45 per hour converts to $88,920 gross per year. That is $1,710 per week, $3,420 per fortnight and about $7,410 per month when annualised.

Why this chart uses 38 hours per week

For Australian full-time employees, the National Employment Standards use 38 hours per week as the maximum weekly-hours reference unless reasonable additional hours apply. Awards, enterprise agreements and individual work arrangements can affect ordinary hours, so your actual schedule may be different.

Employment-hours reference: Fair Work Ombudsman — Maximum weekly hours.

Weekly, fortnightly and monthly pay

The table also shows estimated gross pay for common pay periods. Weekly pay is hourly rate × 38. Fortnightly pay is two weeks of gross pay. Monthly pay is the annualised amount divided by 12, which is useful for comparison but may not match an employer's exact payroll cycle.

When your result will be different

Your actual annual earnings can differ if you work fewer than 52 weeks, have unpaid time away from work, regularly work overtime, receive penalty rates or allowances, or have a different number of ordinary weekly hours. Salary packages can also include components such as superannuation or bonuses that are not represented in this simple gross-pay chart.

How do I convert an hourly rate to annual salary in Australia?

Multiply the hourly rate by your weekly work hours and the number of weeks you work per year. Using 38 hours and 52 weeks, multiply the hourly rate by 1,976.
