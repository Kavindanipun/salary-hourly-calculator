How to use this salary table

Find the annual salary closest to the amount you want to compare. The hourly column assumes 38 hours per week across 52 weeks. Weekly and fortnightly amounts divide the annual salary across 52 weeks, while monthly pay divides it across 12 months.

Why actual payroll amounts can look different

Payroll cycles, rounding, tax withholding and employment conditions can cause payslip amounts to differ from a simple annual conversion. Some employers quote base salary, while others quote a package containing additional components. Use your employment documents when making a final comparison.

Need an exact custom calculation?

The main calculator lets you change weekly hours, weeks per year and work days. That is more appropriate if you work a non-standard schedule.
