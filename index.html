How do I calculate my hourly rate from annual salary?

To figure or work out your hourly pay from an annual salary, divide your annual gross salary by the total number of hours you work in the year.

Hourly rate = Annual salary ÷ (Hours per week × Weeks per year)

Example: $80,000 ÷ (38 × 52) = approximately $40.49 per hour before tax.

If you work a different number of ordinary hours, change the hours-per-week field. If your calculation excludes unpaid periods, change the weeks-per-year field too. This makes the result more useful than assuming every worker has the same schedule.

Why the calculator starts at 38 hours

Fair Work states that the National Employment Standards set a 38-hour weekly reference for full-time employees, with reasonable additional hours possible. Awards, enterprise agreements and individual arrangements can affect actual ordinary hours.

Source: Fair Work Ombudsman — Maximum weekly hours.

How to calculate your hourly rate from monthly salary

If you know your monthly gross salary rather than your annual salary, convert the monthly amount to a yearly amount first, then divide by your annual work hours.

Hourly rate = (Monthly salary × 12) ÷ (Hours per week × Weeks per year)

Example: $6,500/month × 12 = $78,000/year. At 38 hours × 52 weeks, $78,000 ÷ 1,976 ≈ $39.47/hour.

Quick pay-period formulas

  • Yearly → hourly: annual salary ÷ yearly work hours
  • Monthly → hourly: monthly salary × 12 ÷ yearly work hours
  • Weekly → hourly: weekly gross pay ÷ weekly hours
  • Hourly → yearly: hourly rate × weekly hours × weeks per year

Hourly wage to yearly salary formula

To calculate annual salary from an hourly rate, multiply the hourly wage by your weekly hours and the number of weeks you want included in the year.

Annual salary = Hourly rate × Hours per week × Weeks per year

Example: $40/hour × 38 hours × 52 weeks = $79,040 per year before tax.

Salary and hourly rate FAQs

How do I calculate my hourly rate?

Divide your annual gross salary by your total annual work hours. For 38 hours per week across 52 weeks, divide by 1,976. For example, $80,000 ÷ 1,976 is about $40.49 per hour before tax.

How do I figure hourly rate from annual salary?

Use the formula: annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year). Change the weekly hours or weeks if your work pattern is different.

How do I work out hourly pay from annual salary in Australia?

Enter your annual salary and ordinary weekly hours in the calculator. A 38-hour week is provided as an Australian full-time reference, but you can change it to match your employment arrangement.

How do I convert hourly wage to yearly salary?

Multiply your hourly wage by hours worked per week and weeks worked per year. For example, $40 × 38 × 52 = $79,040 per year before tax.

How do I calculate hourly rate from monthly salary?

Multiply monthly gross salary by 12, then divide by annual work hours. At 38 hours per week and 52 weeks, divide the annualised amount by 1,976.

What is the full-time weekly hours reference in Australia?

Fair Work’s National Employment Standards use 38 hours per week as the maximum weekly hours reference for full-time employees, unless reasonable additional hours apply. Actual ordinary hours can vary under an award, agreement or employment arrangement.

Does this salary calculator include tax or superannuation?

No. It is a gross-pay conversion calculator. It does not calculate income tax, superannuation, overtime, penalty rates, allowances or leave loading.

Why can my actual hourly value differ from the result?

Your employment package may include paid leave, superannuation, bonuses, allowances, overtime or other entitlements. Use this tool for gross conversion, not as a payroll or legal-entitlement calculation.
