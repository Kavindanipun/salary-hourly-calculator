Annual salary to hourly rate formula

For a gross salary conversion, divide annual salary by total work hours in the year:

Hourly rate = Annual salary ÷ (weekly hours × weeks per year)

If you use 38 hours per week and 52 weeks per year, the year contains 1,976 work hours for this conversion. An $80,000 annual salary therefore converts to about $40.49 per hour.

Important: this is a mathematical pay conversion, not a determination of your legal base rate or entitlements. Paid leave, overtime, penalties, allowances, superannuation and contract terms can change the practical comparison.

Why 38 hours is commonly used in Australian examples

The National Employment Standards provide a maximum weekly hours reference of 38 hours for a full-time employee unless reasonable additional hours apply. Awards and enterprise agreements can specify or average ordinary hours differently, so the calculator lets you change the default.

For official guidance, see the Fair Work Ombudsman maximum weekly hours fact sheet.

Worked examples

  • $60,000/year: $60,000 ÷ 1,976 ≈ $30.36/hour.
  • $80,000/year: $80,000 ÷ 1,976 ≈ $40.49/hour.
  • $100,000/year: $100,000 ÷ 1,976 ≈ $50.61/hour.
  • $120,000/year: $120,000 ÷ 1,976 ≈ $60.73/hour.
  • $150,000/year: $150,000 ÷ 1,976 ≈ $75.91/hour.

When to change weeks per year

For a simple annual salary comparison, 52 weeks is useful. If you are converting income that is only earned during part of the year, use the number of paid or worked weeks relevant to your scenario. The result should reflect the question you are actually trying to answer.

Salary package versus base salary

Before comparing offers, check whether the advertised figure is base salary or a total remuneration package. A package may include components that are not cash salary. This calculator intentionally does not guess those components.
